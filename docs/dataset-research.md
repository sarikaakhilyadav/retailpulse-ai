# Dataset Research — RetailPulse AI

## Candidate Dataset

**Name:** Online Retail II

**Source:** UCI Machine Learning Repository

**Dataset link:** https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/502/online+retail+ii

**Period:** December 2009 – December 2011

## Business Purpose

Evaluate historical UK retail transactions for sales analysis, product performance, customer behaviour, and sales forecasting.

## Expected Data Fields

- Invoice — Transaction identifier
- StockCode — Product identifier
- Description — Product name
- Quantity — Units purchased
- InvoiceDate — Transaction date
- Price — Unit price
- Customer ID — Customer identifier
- Country — Customer location

## Known Limitations

- Missing customer information may affect customer analysis.
- Cancelled transactions and returns require investigation.
- Inventory costs and profit margins are unavailable.
- Historical data cannot represent current retail demand.
