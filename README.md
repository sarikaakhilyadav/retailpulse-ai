# RetailPulse AI

**Retail Intelligence & Sales Forecasting Platform**

RetailPulse AI is a portfolio project focused on helping retail businesses understand sales performance, customer behaviour, product trends, and future demand through data analytics and forecasting.

## Business Problem

Retail businesses often struggle to turn raw sales data into useful insights for decision-making. RetailPulse AI aims to make these insights easier to understand through interactive dashboards and sales forecasting.

## Planned Dashboards

- **Sales Analytics:** Revenue, sales trends, and key performance indicators.
- **Product Analytics:** Best-selling products, category performance, and product trends.
- **Customer Analytics:** Customer purchasing behaviour and customer segments.
- **AI Sales Forecasting:** Predictive analysis of future sales and demand.

## Project Status

**Phase 1 — Planning and Dataset Research**

Current activities include defining the business problem, documenting dashboard requirements, and researching suitable retail datasets.

## Development Roadmap

1. Dataset selection and validation
2. Data cleaning and exploratory analysis
3. Database design and data preparation
4. Dashboard development
5. Sales forecasting model development
6. Testing, documentation, and deployment
